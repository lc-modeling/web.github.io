    How Payments Work

    Transparent and secure, protecting both students and instructors.

    1

    Pay L&C Models Management

    All student payments are made directly to L&C Models Management — never to an individual instructor.

    2

    Training Is Completed

    Once an instructor successfully delivers the agreed training program, payment is processed by us.

    3

    A Transparent Commission

    A service commission is deducted before the instructor is paid, supporting verification, support, and platform growth — similar to how ride-sharing apps work.
