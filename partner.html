Since 2016 L&C Modeling Academy has trained models for runway, print, pageantry and commercial work across Kenya and beyond. A partnership with us is a direct line to that pipeline — whether you are dressing a show, casting a campaign, sponsoring a cohort of students, or running a corporate-responsibility programme that needs a credible creative partner.

Ways to partner

01

Sponsorship

Back a student cohort, a graduation showcase or a workshop series. Your name travels with every model we put on stage and every piece of coverage the event earns.

02

Collaboration

Co-produce a shoot, a runway segment or a training module with our instructors and talent. You bring the concept; we bring the faces, the walk and the room.

03

CSR & community

Fund scholarships and outreach for models who cannot afford formal training. We handle selection, mentorship and reporting so your impact is documented, not assumed.
