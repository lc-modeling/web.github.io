L&C Modeling Academy ("L&C", "we", "us") runs the L&C mobile app and the website lcmodelingschool.com. We are based in Nairobi, Kenya, and act as the data controller for personal data collected through the app and website.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. By using the app or website you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using them.

4. Sharing your information

We share personal data only with:

  • Authorities when required by law, a court order or the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).
  • A buyer or successor, if L&C is merged, sold or restructured, under the same protections as this policy.

Some providers store data on servers outside Kenya. When that happens we rely on safeguards allowed by section 48 of the Act, such as contractual protections or adequate data protection in the receiving country.

7. Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls limited to authorised staff, and regular backups. No system is fully secure. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify the ODPC within 72 hours and inform you without undue delay, as section 43 of the Act requires.

8. Your rights

Under section 26 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, you can:

  • Be informed of how your data is used
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct data that is false or misleading
  • Delete data that is no longer needed or was unlawfully processed
  • Object to processing, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format

To use any right, email modellinglc@gmail.com. We reply within 14 days and may ask you to confirm your identity. You can also delete your account in the app under Settings.

If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

9. Children

Users under 18 may only register with a parent or guardian's consent, as section 33 of the Act requires. The guardian can access, correct or delete the child's data at any time. Photos of a minor are never shared with agencies or clients without the guardian's written consent. If we learn we hold a minor's data without consent, we delete it.

You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.
